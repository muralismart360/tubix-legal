# Summary
Tubix does not need an account and does not collect your name, email address or phone number. Your game progress (levels, stars, coins, settings) is stored only on your device.

# What the game uses
- Progress and settings are saved on your device only.
- Notifications: if you allow it, the game schedules a local daily reminder on your device. It does not use a server.
- This text is loaded from the internet when you open it, so the game needs a network connection for that.

# Advertising
Tubix may show rewarded video ads that you choose to watch (for example to earn coins) and banner ads on the home and level screens. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use your advertising ID, IP address and device and usage information to show and measure ads. Where the law requires it (for example the EEA and the UK) Tubix asks for your consent first using Google's consent form.
Learn more: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

# Purchases
If in-app purchases are offered, they are processed by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details.

# Children
Tubix is meant for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children.

# Data retention and deletion
Your progress lives on your device. Uninstalling Tubix or clearing its storage removes it. We keep no copy on any server.
