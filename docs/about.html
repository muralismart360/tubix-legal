About Tubix

Tubix is a relaxing liquid colour-sort puzzle for teens and adults. Pour, mix and brew your way through 1000 levels in 20 worlds. Every level is checked so it can always be solved, with no dead ends. Finish customers' potion orders, brew new colours in the Mix Flask, collect tube skins and decorate your lab.

The game is available in English, Telugu and Hindi.
