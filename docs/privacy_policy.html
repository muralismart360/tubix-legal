Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Tubix ("the game") is published by Smart360 ("we"). This policy explains what information the game uses.

Who the game is for

Tubix is intended for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13.

Information we collect

  • Game progress (levels, stars, coins, settings) is stored on your device. It is not sent to us.
  • Advertising ID and ad interaction data are collected by Google AdMob to show and measure ads (rewarded video ads that you choose to watch, and banner ads on the home and level screens). In regions that require it, you are asked for consent first (Google User Messaging Platform).
  • Purchases, if offered, are processed by Google Play. We receive only confirmation that a purchase was made, never your payment details.

What we do not do

We do not sell personal information, do not ask for your name, email, phone number or location, do not use analytics or crash-reporting services, and do not access your contacts, photos or microphone.

Notifications

If you allow it, the game schedules a local reminder every evening at 9:00 pm (you can change the time). It is created on your device (not sent from a server). You can turn it off in Settings > Daily puzzle reminder.

Texts inside the game

The About, How to play, Privacy Policy and Terms screens inside the game load their text from this website, so the game needs an internet connection to refresh them. Nothing about you is sent.

Data deletion

All game data is stored on your device. Uninstall the game or clear its storage to erase it. We keep no copy on any server.

Third-party services

Google AdMob and Google Play Services. Their policies: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
